# VPN connection troubleshooting

If the VPN connection fails with error 809, confirm your internet connection works without the VPN. Restart the VPN client and verify the configured gateway is vpn.example.test. Capture the error code and contact IT if it persists. Do not disable the firewall.

For VPN authentication failures, check the device clock and complete the MFA prompt. If the account is locked, contact the service desk for identity verification. Never share passwords or one-time codes.
